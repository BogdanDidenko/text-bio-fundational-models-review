# Immersion log (2026-10-14)

Raw observations from reading all 54 papers' input dossiers (`immersion_dossier.md`: every route with
its chain, operations and stated parameter values). These notes are unfiltered: they are leads to
quantify, not findings.

## Leads

L1 Biology without measurements. In several papers the "biological" input is only names, identifiers
or text knowledge about biology, not measured data. Examples:
- gene-name prompts in Geneverse rec_003043 and DeepSeek-BFT rec_003008 ("Tell me about gene CTSL");
- entity/fact templates in GP-GPT rec_003131;
- gene/disease-name prompts in OCellus rec_000138;
- PubMed and structured corpora in XunZi-R and BioMedGPT-LM;
- gene summaries entering as GenePT text embeddings (X-Cell, Bio-BLIP, P3GPT).
To quantify: share of biological-payload inputs that are measurements versus names or text knowledge.

L2 Verbalised transcriptomes keep a few hundred genes at most:
- top 50 genes (CellWhisperer rec_000827) and top 100 (Cell2Sentence rec_003206, CellHermes rec_001126, TeamPath rec_003323);
- top 128 (BIOVERSE) and top 134 (OmicsLM rec_000246);
- top 200 (CellTosg2Sequence) and top 50/40/30 differential genes (GEMGen rec_003629);
- top 5 marker genes per neighbour (OCellus EvenClock).
Ranking drops magnitudes. Encoder routes keep more: top 2,048 for scGPT/scMMGPT, and full 20,541-gene
vectors for OmicsLM. To quantify: K distribution and the share of the transcriptome kept.

L3 Synthetic language. Text paired with biology is often written by general LLMs, and sometimes
verified manually:
- conversations and annotations: CellWhisperer (GPT-4, Mixtral) and C2S-Scale (captions by GPT-4o, QA by GPT-4.5);
- rephrased descriptions: PROCYON (OpenAI models);
- mechanistic interpretations: XunZi;
- synthetic gene descriptions: Geneverse; samples written by GPT-OSS-120B in rec_003008.
To quantify: papers whose text supervision is generated by another LLM.

L4 Biology enters as other models' embeddings. Many encoder routes start from a third-party pretrained
model (Geneformer, ESM-2/3, Evo2, AlphaGenome, UNI, DINOv2, TranscriptFormer, CellWhisperer,
GenePT/ada, GraphMVP, BLIP-2); Bio-BLIP uses only precomputed embeddings.
To quantify: share of biological encoder routes that depend on a named external FM, and the dependency network.

L5 Adaptation sits in a thin connector. The usual stated setup is frozen encoder + trainable
linear/MLP projector, and the LLM is sometimes frozen (Bio-BLIP, BIOVERSE alignment). Q-Formers are
trained in pretraining and then frozen. To quantify from stated parameter_state/trainability values.

L6 Text arrives late. Pretraining routes are often biology-only (symbol streams, omics, images); text
instructions enter in fine-tuning or evaluation (OmniNA, Omni-DNA, scMIR captions are an exception).
To quantify: text roles by lifecycle phase, within papers.

L7 The text interface doubles as a benchmark interface. Verbalised inputs let unchanged general LLMs
(GPT-4o, o1, o3-mini, Llama, Qwen in Cell-o1 rec_001617) be evaluated on biology. Single-cell becomes a
reasoning benchmark for any LLM.

L8 Carriers are not dictated by modality; cross-carrier conversions occur:
- DNA rendered as page images then OCR'd (OpticalDNA rec_001830);
- variant pileups as RGB images (Med-PaLM M);
- MNIST images as VQ tokens, mapped from DNA (Omni-DNA);
- graphs as text templates or KG triples (CellHermes, CellTosg2Sequence);
- DNA "translated" into English (Human Genome Book rec_002243).
To quantify: routes whose carrier family differs from the source's native form.

L9 Generative models used as scorers: log-likelihood differences for variant effects (OmniNA,
GenNA) and perplexity over answers (CellWhisperer, GenNA).

L10 Corpus structure caveats:
- Version pairs: OmniNA 2024/2026; CellWhisperer bioRxiv/journal (rec_000827/rec_002105); Cell2Text 2025/2026 (rec_001838/rec_000248).
- Many atlas "models" are comparators (GPT-4o, o1 and Llama variants in Cell-o1 and Geneverse; CatBoost in P3GPT).
Statistics must be at study level and separate proposed models from comparators.

L11 Spatial context is serialised as text order (TISSUENARRATOR proximity traversal, OCellus EvenClock
sectors, C2S-Scale neighbourhoods): geometry becomes word order.

L12 Many routes stop at a named learned module; their information content is decided by an external model.
