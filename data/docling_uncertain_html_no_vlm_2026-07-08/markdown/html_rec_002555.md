# Let the data speak - single-cell analysis with CellWhisperer

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) captures the state of thousands of cells simultaneously, establishing it as a cornerstone assay in modern biomedical research. Yet, interpreting the resulting data relies on specialized software tools and on writing code. As a result, scRNA-seq analysis has been largely confined to bioinformaticians, creating a bottleneck in scientific workflows. The emergence of multimodal language models, such as ChatGPT and Gemini, offers a perspective to overcome this obstacle through intuitive, language-based data exploration.

CellWhisperer is particularly valuable for initial data exploration, as it provides immediate insights from raw read count profiles. In a comparison with conventional bioinformatics analysis of colon cells, CellWhisperer reached similar conclusions four times faster. It also enabled the mapping of organ formation over time in a human development atlas and identified previously unknown developmental markers for the heart and other organs. These findings underscore the potential of CellWhisperer to capture new and compelling biological insights.
