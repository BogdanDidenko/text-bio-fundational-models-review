# OpenAlex Works Search API

Endpoint: `https://api.openalex.org/works`

Main `search` value:

```text
(("single cell" OR "single-cell" OR scRNA-seq OR "RNA-seq" OR "RNA seq" OR "gene expression" OR scATAC-seq OR "ATAC-seq" OR "chromatin accessibility" OR CITE-seq OR "spatial transcriptomics" OR "multi-omics" OR "multi omics" OR multiomics OR transcriptomics OR genomics OR proteomics OR epigenomics OR "cell type" OR "gene regulatory") AND ("language model" OR LLM OR "large language model" OR "natural language" OR GPT OR CLIP OR "cross-modal" OR multimodal OR "multi-modal" OR tokenization OR "gene token" OR prompt OR "text generation" OR "cell-to-text" OR "text-to-cell" OR NLP) AND ("foundation model" OR pretrained OR "pre-trained" OR "self-supervised" OR transformer OR generative OR decoder OR autoregressive OR "transfer learning" OR "zero-shot" OR "few-shot" OR "masked modeling" OR "attention mechanism" OR "deep learning"))
```

Supplementary model-name `search` value:

```text
(scGPT OR tGPT OR LangCell OR ChatCell OR CellWhisperer OR GenePT OR GeneGPT OR CellPLM OR Nicheformer OR EpiAgent OR PathOmCLIP)
```

Incremental filters are generated from the run interval:

```text
from_publication_date:YYYY-MM-DD,to_publication_date:YYYY-MM-DD,language:en,is_oa:true
```

The main query uses OpenAlex's field-scoped `title_and_abstract.search` filter;
the model-name query uses `title.search`. This prevents the general OpenAlex
`search` parameter from retrieving records matched only in full text. Both
searches use cursor pagination. Their union is deduplicated by OpenAlex work ID
before the cross-database deduplication stage, while per-query membership is
retained in the raw and normalized exports.
